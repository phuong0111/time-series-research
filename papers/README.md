| `1810.02266v1.pdf` | Concept-drifting Data Streams are Time Series; The Case for Continuous Adaptation | Jesse Read | 2018-10-04 | [1810.02266v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.02266v1) |

**1810.02266v1** — cs.LG / stat.ML. Argues that the standard data-stream assumption of
independently distributed instances is contradictory under concept drift: drift necessarily
implies temporal dependence, so a drifting stream *is* a time series. Concludes that
Hoeffding-tree ensembles, the dominant stream-learning approach, are not naturally suited
to learning *within* drift.
